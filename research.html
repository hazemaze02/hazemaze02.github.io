Research

Lab affiliations, fellowship, and work in progress.

Labs

Graduate Research Assistant, Columbia University Irving Medical Center, under Prof. Yading Yuan (Department of Radiation Oncology, also affiliated with Columbia's Data Science Institute). My work focuses on extending Gaussian Splatting-based neural rendering to 3D volumetric medical imaging; currently, I'm exploring how to construct multi-basis material images directly from conventional, single-energy CT scans, rather than relying on specialized dual-energy hardware.

Graduate Research Assistant, Computer Graphics and User Interfaces Lab, Columbia University, under Prof. Steve Feiner. Contributing to a project that uses AR/VR headsets and 3D Gaussian Splatting to support remote technical assistance, reconstructing a piece of faulty hardware captured on a headset into a realistic 3D model a remote technician can inspect instead of flat video.

Graduate Research Assistant, Geometry and the City Lab, under Prof. Silvia Sellán. The lab is dedicated to geometry processing, with a particular focus on applications with positive real-world impact. I contribute to a project on foveated geometry, which renders 3D meshes at full detail only where the viewer is looking and at lower detail elsewhere, cutting the rendering load for compute-constrained devices like wearables and AR/VR headsets. The approach splits a mesh into patches and arranges them hierarchically by level of detail, so the renderer can pick the right resolution for each region in real time.

Graduate Research Assistant, Laboratory of Neural Engineering and Control, Columbia University, under Dr. Qi Wang. Investigating the coupling between pupil dilation dynamics and EEG patterns, working toward real-time BCI systems that can detect and respond to fluctuations in focus and cognitive engagement in adaptive learning environments.
